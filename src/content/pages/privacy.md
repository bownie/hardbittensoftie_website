Richard W. Bown is the data controller for this website, richardwbownauthor.com.

## The newsletter

If you sign up for the newsletter, I collect your email address and use it to send you occasional news about my books.

- **What I collect:** your email address, and the date you signed up.
- **Why:** to send the newsletter you asked for. Nothing else.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given when you submit the form.
- **Who processes it:** MailerLite, the email marketing provider that stores the list on my behalf, under its own privacy notice.
- **Confirmation:** signups use double opt-in. You'll get one email asking you to confirm, and until you click the link in it you are not on the list.
- **How long:** until you unsubscribe.
- **Unsubscribing:** every email has an unsubscribe link, and clicking it takes you off the list.

## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages get read. **It does not load until you accept it.** The banner sets one preference in your browser; if you decline, no analytics request is made at all.

- **What it collects:** the pages you visit, your approximate location derived from your IP address, your browser and device type, and a random identifier stored in a cookie.
- **Cookies:** `_ga` and `_ga_*`, set by Google. The `_ga` cookie lasts up to two years by default.
- **Legal basis:** your consent. Declining costs you nothing and the site works exactly the same.
- **Changing your mind:** use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer at any time and choose Decline. That stops further collection; it doesn't delete what was already collected.
- **Who processes it:** Google Ireland Limited, under Google's privacy terms.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes standard web requests — IP address, browser, requested page — as part of serving the files. I don't have access to those logs.

## Links to other sites

The book sites, Bluesky, LinkedIn and Goodreads have their own privacy notices. Once you follow a link away from this site, this notice no longer applies.
